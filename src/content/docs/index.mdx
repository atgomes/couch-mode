## What you get

- **Console boot:** power on the TV and the PC is in a fullscreen launcher a few seconds later.
- **No login screen:** no password, no keyboard.
- **One library:** Steam, Epic and GOG in a single controller-friendly UI.
- **Play from anywhere:** stream to a phone or Steam Deck, with the picture sized correctly for each screen.
